How it works

One WhatsApp message, up to three platforms. Every step is code in the repo.

01 · CAPTURE

You send a thought

A voice note or a text to your Fubuu WhatsApp number. Twilio forwards it to the backend.

POST /whatsapp-webhook
02 · UNDERSTAND

Fubuu transcribes it

Audio becomes text with Whisper. Your plan and daily limits are checked in Redis.

FFmpeg · Whisper · Redis
03 · WRITE

AI writes the post

Your bio and optional prompt shape a hook-first post for each platform.

OpenAI · your prompt
04 · PUBLISH

Posted and confirmed

Fubuu posts with your connected accounts, then replies on WhatsApp with what went out.

X · LinkedIn · Threads

Set up once, in five steps

The dashboard walks each new user through everything Fubuu needs, and ticks each step off as it is done.

  • Connect X, LinkedIn and Threads with OAuth (via Clerk)
  • Add the WhatsApp number you will talk from
  • Describe yourself so posts sound like you
  • Optionally rewrite the prompt itself
  • Send your first voice note

Written like you, per platform

Short and hook-first for X and Threads, a fuller post with a question for LinkedIn. Daily limits per platform keep your feed sane, and three gentle prompts a day, in your time zone, keep the habit going.

Works on the phone you already hold

There is no new app to open. Users talk to WhatsApp; the web app is only for setup, settings and billing (Stripe checkout and customer portal).

Run it on your machine

Docker, Node 20+ and uv. Demo mode needs no accounts: every outside service is a local stand-in and no message leaves your machine.

# clone and start everything
git clone \
